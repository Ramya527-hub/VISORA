Final Recommendation

VISORA is a prototype clinical decision-support concept. Final spectacle selection must be made by a qualified optometrist after appropriate clinical assessment.

Privacy & Ethics

VISORA follows a privacy-by-design concept: collect only necessary information, obtain consent before using patient photos/data, restrict access to authorised users, provide deletion/withdrawal options, and keep the optometrist in control of the final decision.

This front-end demonstration does not provide real clinical AI, secure server storage, or production authentication.
